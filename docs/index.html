CoSE Arcade — original lunar & Martian games

Original browser games built from scratch in the spirit of the classic 1990s Maxis simulation games, with a lunar-settlement theme — vanilla JavaScript, no build step, no downloads. Click a card and play.

Lunar Arcade — Lunar Habitat

Maxis-tribute browser games, lunar-themed and dependency-free. The flagship Lunar Habitat is a vertical colony builder that inverts the tower-management genre: you build up 26 levels and dig down 24 into the regolith, where the surface is the most dangerous floor in the building. Also includes Boring Mining.

Lunar Sims — Lunar Farm

Static-HTML management sims on the lunar surface. In Lunar Farm you run the farm feeding a lava-tube colony at the Marius Hills skylight: drag out grow halls up to 8×8, pick a crop per hall, and balance yield, lighting draw and water against each hall's footprint.

live Play → Code
🎲

Settlers of the Moon or Mars

A choose-your-own-adventure that blends Settlers of Catan, Dungeons & Dragons and SimCity to teach space biology, biotechnology and aerospace engineering. Play it three ways — in the browser, as an AI-guided tabletop role-play, or as a 3D-printed board.

live Play → Code
🏈

Brute Bowl

A planner and visualiser for a Blood Bowl-style fantasy-sport match (Halfling vs Undead), using LLMs to help design teams and characters and to reason about plays — special-move tables, dice-probability interactions and the tactical spectacle of the field.

live Play → Code
⚔️

Lunar Red Alert

A space-age total conversion of OpenRA — the libre RTS engine behind the classic Westwood games — reimagining Command & Conquer: Red Alert for a lunar/vacuum setting. A desktop mod (runs on OpenRA, not in the browser); the page is a landing/overview.

All original works — third-party engine forks (habitat-sim, mars-sim, Lunar_Red_Alert_fork/OpenRA) are excluded. Games marked live run in the browser; desktop mod runs outside it.
